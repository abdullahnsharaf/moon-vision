How bright is it?

Three independent estimates. Lower magnitude means brighter; lux is the light it casts on the ground.

From the phase

The standard brightness range for every Moon of this phase (Allen's lunar phase law). Simple, but every crescent gets the same answer.

From the pixels

Measures the lit pixels inside the outline. Depends on your camera's exposure; turns into real units when the photo stores its shutter, aperture and ISO.

Photometric model

Turns the measured lit fraction into a phase angle, then into brightness. Follows the photo like method B, ignores camera settings like method A.
